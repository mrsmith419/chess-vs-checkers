The room creator plays chess. The person who joins plays checkers.

How to play
  • Players alternate turns: one chess move, then one checkers move.
  • Chess pieces move in their usual directions and can capture checkers.
  • Checkers move diagonally forward, jump over chess pieces to capture, and become kings at the far edge.
  • Checkers kings move and capture diagonally in either direction.
  • Chess pawns promote to queens on the far edge.
  • The chess king can be captured. The first side to lose every piece loses.

Note: This crossover uses standard piece movement, but does not enforce chess check/checkmate, castling, or en passant. Checkers captures are mandatory when available.
